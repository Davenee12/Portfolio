How we work

A four-phase engagement,
shaped around your academic calendar.

Higher ed runs on terms, not sprints. Our process respects census dates, board cycles, and reporting deadlines — and produces artifacts your team owns at the end.

01

DISCOVER

Stakeholder interviews, data inventory, and a clear scoping doc. We map what exists, what's broken, and what the board actually wants to see.

02

MODEL

SQL models in Git, semantic layer documented, FERPA review baked in. Every metric defined once, used everywhere.

03

BUILD

Dashboards, tools, or both — iterated weekly with your IR team in the loop. No black-box deliverables.

04

HANDOFF

Documentation, training, and a 30-day support runway. Your team owns it. We're a phone call away when you need us.
